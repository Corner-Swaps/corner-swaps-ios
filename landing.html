Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about safety, rules, or how the platform operates? Here are details for active neighbors.

Yes, Barterland is completely free. Our platform is built as a non-monetized local directory to stimulate neighborhood resilience, promote eco-friendly resource sharing, and build community relationships. No credit cards or transaction fees are ever requested.
We ask all neighbors to commit to our Safety Pledge. You must meet only in safe, public, and well-lit local hubs (such as parks, coffee shops, or libraries) indicated on the village map. Never share your exact apartment number or home address, and never meet in private residential spaces.
You can list any clean, safe, and legal household items (power tools, puzzles, camping gear, books) or offer any friendly service or tutoring (gardening assistance, baking lessons, acoustic guitar coaching, basic coding help). Harassment, dangerous objects, commercial listings, or paid services are strictly banned.
Every time you complete a trade inside the chat window, you earn reputation XP (Experience Points) and level up. After a swap, you can vouch for your neighbor. High vouches and positive swap stats display on your profile, letting neighbors trade with confidence.

Privacy Policy · Terms of Service

Privacy Policy

1. Our Commitment to Privacy
At Barterland, we value the trust you place in us. We operate as a cashless community exchange, and we are committed to protecting your personal information. Unlike commercial platforms, we do not trade, sell, or rent your data to advertisers.

2. Information We Collect
We only collect minimal data necessary to function, including your email address, an approximate neighborhood area, and optional user profile items (e.g. your name and level details). We deliberately offset map pins to keep your exact home address confidential.

3. Use of Information
Your coordinates and item descriptions are displayed locally on the community map to enable safe neighbor trades. Real-time chat messages are stored locally in your browser storage whenever possible and are never monitored.

Terms of Service

1. Cashless Exchange Policy
Barterland is 100% cashless. The posting of cash listings, requests for credit card fees, interest-bearing loans, or commercial trading is strictly prohibited. Any neighbor attempting commercial transactions will be suspended.

2. Safety & Code of Conduct
All exchanges must occur in public, well-lit safety zones. Neighbors must treat each other with respect, honor agreed-upon swaps, and ensure items are clean and safe for usage. Dangerous goods, illegal services, and harassment are entirely banned.

3. Disclaimer of Liability
Barterland is a local directory tool. We do not inspect trade items or verify neighbor identities. Swaps are performed at your own risk. Neighbors are responsible for completing trades safely.
